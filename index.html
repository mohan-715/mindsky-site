About MindSky

Lessons you can see, change and test.

What it is

MindSky teaches Physics, Maths, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science and Indian Geography & History for Grades 6 to 12. Every topic is a star on a map. Open one and you get a short story, a clear explanation, an experiment you can change yourself, and a quiz that tells you why each answer is right or wrong.

Your progress

There are no accounts. Your discovered topics and quiz scores are saved inside this browser on this device, and nowhere else. If you clear your browser data they are gone, so use the backup code on the Profile page to keep a copy or to move to another device.

Privacy

  • MindSky does not collect your name, email or answers, and has no ads or trackers.
  • Nothing you do in a lesson is sent to any server.
  • All fonts and files are served from this site itself, so nothing is loaded from other companies. The web host may keep ordinary visit logs, like any website.

Using it offline

After you have opened MindSky once with internet, it keeps working without a connection. You can also install it like an app.

  • Android (Chrome): menu, then "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • iPhone (Safari): Share button, then "Add to Home Screen".
  • Computer (Chrome or Edge): the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
